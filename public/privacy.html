CashFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

CashFlow is a personal finance app for tracking your money and splitting expenses with friends and groups. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy
Account details: name, phone number or Google email (depending on how you sign in), optional username, profile photo.To create and secure your account and let you find and link with friends.
Financial data you enter: accounts and balances, transactions (amount, category, date, time, notes, descriptions, income source), scheduled transactions.This is the core of the app: tracking, reports and balances.
Split data: people and groups you add, splits, shares and settlements.To calculate who owes whom and sync shared splits with linked users.
Contacts (only if you allow the permission): read from your phone.To help you add people to CashFlow. People you add are saved to your account.
Camera and photos (only if you allow): receipt images and profile pictures.To attach receipts to transactions and set your photo.
Location (optional, only if you allow): coordinates you attach to a transaction.To show where a transaction happened. Never collected in the background.
Notifications settings and reminders.To send the reminders and alerts you turn on.
Feedback you send: name, email, message, attachments.To fix problems and improve the app.
Technical data: basic logs such as IP address and device type kept by our hosting providers.To run and secure the service.

2. How we use it

We use your information only to provide CashFlow: show your data, calculate balances and reports, sync shared splits, send notifications you enabled, handle support requests and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

3. Who can see your data

  • You. The app is built so each user can only read their own accounts, transactions and categories.
  • People you link with. When you split an expense with another CashFlow user, they can see that split: its description, amounts, shares, who paid, and settlements, plus your name and profile photo. Your phone number is shared only according to your privacy settings.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Vercel (hosting), Resend (emailing feedback to us) and Google (if you use Google sign-in). They process data only to provide their service.
  • The CashFlow developer. Your data is stored so the app can calculate balances and sync splits, so it is not end-to-end encrypted. We do not browse user data. We access it only when needed for support you request, to keep the service secure, or when the law requires.

4. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with encryption at rest by our providers. Access to the database is restricted by per-user rules, and our admin accounts are protected. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, transactions, accounts, categories, people, groups and uploaded files. Deletion is blocked while you have unsettled splits, so that other people's balances stay correct. Other users who saved you as a contact will keep a contact entry labelled "Deleted user", and settled history they share with you may remain on their side.

You can also request deletion without the app: see how to delete your account and data. Copies in provider backups are removed on the providers' normal schedule.

6. Your choices

  • Allow or deny contacts, camera, photos, location and notification permissions at any time in your phone's settings. The app still works without them.
  • Control who can see your phone number and profile in the app's privacy settings.
  • Export your transactions from the app's Data & Backup settings.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information by emailing us.

7. Children

CashFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for major changes we will tell you in the app.
