Delete your CashFlow account and data

App: CashFlow (package app.cashflow.mobile)

Option 1: Delete inside the app (instant)

  1. Open CashFlow and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account and type DELETE to confirm.

If the app says you have unsettled splits, settle them first (or ask the other person to), then try again. This protects other people's balances.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email cashflow.app.dev@gmail.com with the subject CashFlow account deletion request. Send it from the email linked to your account, or include the phone number you signed up with, so we can confirm it is you. We will delete the account and reply, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, name, phone number, email and username
  • Your accounts, balances, transactions and scheduled transactions
  • Your categories, people and groups
  • Your uploaded files: profile photos and receipt images

What may remain

  • Other CashFlow users who saved you as a contact keep that contact entry, relabelled "Deleted user". Shared split history on their side may remain so their own records stay correct.
  • Feedback messages you emailed to us may be kept for support records.
  • Copies in our providers' backups are removed on their normal schedule.

See also our Privacy Policy.
